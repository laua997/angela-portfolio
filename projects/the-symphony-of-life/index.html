project overview

Year
2024
Medium
Wire, vinyl, oil paint, soft pastel, magazines
Size
25x25x42in
Category
3D

This piece reflects the complex relationship between memory and experience. I believe that memories are the foundation of who we are, yet as time passes they become harder to grasp—especially the older ones, weathered and faded. The record player in this work symbolizes the mind, weighed down by the sheer volume of memories and experiences. Each experience is like a record, stored in albums that represent different chapters of our lives marked by distinct emotions and lessons learned.

At some point, the record player may snap, unable to hold onto the disks, symbolizing moments of disconnection from our pasts. But as long as my record player holds, I will always have a disk re/playing, a poignant reminder to cherish time and fleeting experiences.
