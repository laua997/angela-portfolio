project overview

Year
2024
Medium
Oil on canvas
Size
Each 64x48in
Category
2D

These two pieces depict different types of memory. The left combines elements from my home and friends' homes into a composite room, where each corner holds a timeless interaction. The vibrant colors detach the viewer from my specific memories, inviting them to reflect on their own. The right portrays an imagined memory of a prom I never attended, rendered in vibrant pinks and deep purples. Faceless figures in elegant attire convey the surreal quality of an experience I could only dream of, while a single black-and-white figure with a blank cutout represents me: a shadow observing from afar, absent from the tangible reality of the scene.
